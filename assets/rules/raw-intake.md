# Raw Intake

Raw preserves source material and evidence, not AI-authored knowledge. Use existing source bundles and manifests under `raw/YYYY/MM/DD/<source>/`; do not invent another top-level catalog.

Register the source URI/path, available title, version and section/timestamp locators first. Read or extract relevant parts when a task needs them; reuse existing artifacts by content hash. Registration alone is not a full read. For unstable sources where reproducibility matters, retain a permitted snapshot.

A repository, video or article need not produce a Wiki page. Propose reusable conclusions in Draft only when supported by evidence and scope. Keep generated interpretation out of Raw; preserve original or mechanically converted content and partial/failed/skipped states. Execution traces remain provenance, not ordinary knowledge.

The current legacy episodic recall scans Markdown and JSONL; a source manifest is not automatically a searchable metadata index. Do not claim a new index or reduced cost without implementation and measurement.
